HNPulse

A clean, fast, and beautiful Hacker News reader for iOS.

Features

📰

All Story Types

Browse Top, New, Best, Ask HN, Show HN, and Jobs — all in one place.

💬

Threaded Comments

Color-coded depth indicators, collapse/expand threads, and user profiles.

🔍

Powerful Search

Search stories with filters for sort order and time range via Algolia.

🔖

Bookmarks

Save stories for later reading. All bookmarks stay on your device.

🎨

Themes & Customization

Light, Dark, Sepia, Black (OLED), adjustable font sizes, and compact mode.

🔒

Privacy First

Zero data collection. No analytics, no tracking, no ads. Your data stays on your device.
